The 2027 season is already well under way, so it seems worth going back over 2026 one more time.

By the numbers, the season was close to excellent. My best time was 14.10, the second fastest of my career and only two hundredths of a second off my personal best. The average of my three best races, 14.13, was the best I have ever had, and my average across the whole season was only six thousandths slower than in 2024, my best season so far. My top speed of 35.79 km/h was also my fastest in three years. At the time of writing I am seventh in the world rankings.

Even so, the feeling the season leaves behind is mostly frustration. My personal best did not improve, close as I came.

![Esa-Pekka Mattila and Leo-Pekka Tähti racing in the rain on a blue track.](/images/season-2026-race.jpg)

The other thing that added to the frustration was that the season had no clear main target. The European Championships were cancelled in early spring, and there were no other championships this year. I am still second in the European rankings, though.

Over the season it has also become concrete that my career is nearing its end. The next two years are my last as an active athlete, which leaves the World Championships in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, in June 2027 and the Paralympic Games in Los Angeles in 2028.

I have been in the world top eight for the past four years, and the goal is to move into the top three. So the chase for a new personal best, and for a World Championship and Paralympic medal, goes on.

![Esa-Pekka Mattila and Leo-Pekka Tähti analysing on a tablet beside the track.](/images/season-2026-trackside.jpg)
